batu advises owners and investors on commercial real estate across three connected disciplines: advisory, valuation and deal origination. Advisory means working through a client's position before recommending a course: what to hold, what to sell, what to buy and when.

Valuation is grounded in evidence: location, demand, comparable transactions and realistic income. Deal origination builds on it, finding and shaping opportunities, including situations that are not publicly marketed.

Within commercial real estate, hotels hold a dedicated place at batu. A hotel is both a property and an operating business, and its value sits in the relationship between the two.

The practice covers hotel sales and acquisitions for owners and investors, and advice on hotel management agreements: the contracts between owner and operator that shape how an asset performs long after the deal is done.

batu also works with unique estates: properties whose character, history or setting makes them difficult to compare and impossible to repeat.

These assets rarely belong on a public portal. The work happens off market, through quiet research and direct approaches, giving sellers privacy and control over who knows a property is available.
